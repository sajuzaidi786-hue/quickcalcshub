Wadsworth Center Lab Tops Out: $1.7B Albany Project Explained

Gilbane-Turner topped out the $1.7B Wadsworth Center Laboratory in Albany. Key facts, what topping out means, and how steel-frame buildings use concrete.

Updated October 25, 2026. Based on public announcements from New York State, DASNY, Turner and trade press. This is our own summary.

The new Wadsworth Center Laboratory in Albany, New York has reached its highest point. The design-build team of Gilbane Building Company and Turner Construction Company, working with architect HOK, finished the structural steel frame of a $1.7 billion public health laboratory, and New York's governor announced the milestone on October 14, 2026.

Key facts

ProjectWadsworth Center Laboratory, Harriman State Office Campus, Albany
Cost$1.7 billion
SizeFive stories, about 663,000 sq ft
BuildersGilbane Building Co. and Turner Construction JV
DesignHOK
Owner and deliveryNew York State Department of Health, through DASNY
SteelOver 8,200 major components, about 5,545 tons (per Turner)

What topping out means

Topping out is the moment the last, highest structural beam goes into place. Crews usually sign it before it is lifted, and projects often add a ceremony. It shows the frame is complete. Roofing, walls, mechanical systems and the interior still follow.

Why this project is a big deal

  • Largest in DASNY history. It is the biggest construction project the state's construction authority has undertaken.
  • Five sites become one. The building will bring five separate Capital Region facilities together and house nearly 800 staff.
  • Public health work. The Wadsworth Center covers areas such as infectious disease surveillance, wastewater testing, environmental health, newborn screening, diagnostics and laboratory oversight, and it was founded in 1901.
  • A fully electric design. HOK describes it as a flexible, fully electric facility.

How the budget grew

When the Gilbane-Turner team was first selected in 2022, the state described the laboratory as a $750 million project. The current figure is $1.7 billion. Spread over 663,000 sq ft, that is roughly $2,560 per square foot, but this is only a rough average because the budget covers far more than the building shell, including laboratory systems, utilities and site work.

Where concrete fits in a steel-frame building

The announcements focus on steel, but steel-frame buildings still use a lot of concrete: foundations below, and floors that are commonly concrete slabs poured over metal deck. Frequently asked questions

What does topping out mean in construction?

It marks the placement of the highest structural beam. Crews often sign the final beam before it is lifted, and many projects hold a ceremony. It is a milestone, not the end of construction.

Who is building the Wadsworth Center Laboratory?

A joint venture of Gilbane Building Company and Turner Construction Company is the design-build team, with HOK as design partner. The Dormitory Authority of the State of New York (DASNY) is delivering it for the state Department of Health.

How big is the new laboratory?

It is a five-story building of about 663,000 square feet on the Harriman State Office Campus in Albany, designed to bring five existing sites together.

Do steel-frame buildings use concrete?

Yes. Foundations are concrete, and floors are commonly concrete slabs poured over metal deck on the steel frame. The announcements reviewed do not give this project's slab details.
