Frequently asked questions

How many 80 lb bags of concrete make one cubic yard?

About 45 bags. One 80 lb bag yields roughly 0.60 cubic feet, and a cubic yard is 27 cubic feet.

Should I add extra for waste?

Yes. Most contractors add 5% to 10% for uneven ground, spillage and over-excavation.

When should I order ready-mix instead of bags?

Above about 1 cubic yard (roughly 45 bags), ready-mix is usually cheaper and easier. Many suppliers charge a short-load fee under 3 to 4 yards, so ask first.
