Concrete Slab Thickness: What You Need for Patios, Driveways and Garages

Choose the right thickness for each project, and see how thickness changes the amount of concrete.

Thickness is the biggest factor in how much concrete you buy. Every extra inch on a 10 x 10 ft slab adds about 0.31 cubic yards.

ProjectTypical thickness
Walkway, patio4 in
Shed base4 to 6 in
Driveway, garage floor5 to 6 in
Heavy vehicles6 in or more, engineer-specified

These are general figures. Soil, climate and local code can change them, so confirm with your building department. Reinforcement (wire mesh or rebar) and a compacted gravel base matter as much as thickness.
