About QuickCalcs Hub

Free, simple concrete estimating tools.

QuickCalcs Hub offers free calculators that turn project dimensions into cubic yards, bag counts and cost estimates for slabs, footings, columns and post holes.

Results are estimates based on standard bag yields (80 lb = 0.60 ft³, 60 lb = 0.45 ft³, 40 lb = 0.30 ft³). Always confirm with your supplier. The site is supported by advertising.
