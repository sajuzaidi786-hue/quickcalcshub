How this calculator works

A concrete mix ratio such as 1:2:4 means 1 part cement, 2 parts sand and 4 parts gravel by volume. The dry materials take up more space than the finished concrete, so the calculator multiplies the wet volume by about 1.54 to get the dry volume, then splits it by the ratio.

Example: 1 cubic yard of 1:2:4 concrete

1 yard = 27 ft³ wet. Dry volume = 27 x 1.54 = 41.6 ft³. Divide by 7 parts: cement 5.9 ft³ (about 6 bags of 94 lb), sand 11.9 ft³, gravel 23.8 ft³.

Notes

  • One 94 lb bag of cement is about 1 cubic foot.
  • Real mixes vary with the sand, gravel and water used, so treat the result as an estimate and add a margin.
  • Pre-mixed bags are easier and more consistent for small jobs. Use the concrete calculator for bag counts.
  • For structural work, follow an engineer's specification.
